# Referential quick reference

Routing aid for the official referentials. The NR-* identifiers of Green Vibe
are project rules, not official criterion numbers. A thematic relation is not
evidence that an official criterion is satisfied: for formal assessments,
record the edition, the exact criterion, applicability, test procedure and
evidence, from the official text.

| Referential | Use for | Relation to Green Vibe rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RGESN 2024 (78 criteria, 9 families; ARCEP/ARCOM/ADEME) | Environmental design of digital services: strategy, specifications, architecture, UX, content, frontend, backend, hosting, algorithms | The `rgesn` field of a rule points at the related criterion when one applies; family coverage spans the 9 families |
| GR491 (INR) | Software and service design decisions across the lifecycle | Cross-cutting: strategy, architecture, UX, frontend, backend, hosting |
| RGAA (versioned) | Accessibility verification: keyboard, focus, semantics, alt text, contrast, labels | NR-UX-001, NR-LANG-HTML-003; keep the RGAA acronym and identify the version |
| Opquast | Web quality: understandable content, reliable navigation, forms, errors, security basics | NR-UX-006, NR-API-006, NR-LANG-HTML-003 |

## Citation discipline

- Cite a referential only when the evidence supports the reference.
- Never state or imply certification: the plugin guides and audits, the formal
  audit remains a human exercise with its own procedure.
- When quoting a person or attributing a statement, require a verifiable
  primary source linked to the exact claim; without one, present the content
  as project guidance and drop the attribution.
