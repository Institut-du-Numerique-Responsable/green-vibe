# The 3U test: utile, utilisé, utilisable

Use these three questions when the value or readiness of a service, feature or AI
pilot is in scope. This is a decision aid, not a certification, and not a reason to
audit every small change. Related rules: NR-SERVICE-001, NR-SERVICE-002, NR-AI-001.

| Question | Feasible check | What the evidence supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Utile - does it help? | Compare a small set of representative tasks with the current process or a simple non-AI alternative. Count correct outcomes and total time including correction. | Record task, baseline, sample, criteria. Faster but worse quality does not establish utility. |
| Utilisé - is it used when needed? | Over a relevant period, count people with a real opportunity to use it, those who try, and those who return for another need. Ask a few users and non-users why. | Keep numerators, denominators and period. Downloads, logins or mandated use do not establish adoption. Before launch, usage is unverified, not failed. |
| Utilisable - can people complete the task? | Observe a few intended users doing the essential journey without coaching: keyboard, assistive tech, errors, realistic device and network. | Record completion, blockers, recovery. A reproducible keyboard blocker is a failure for that journey. Automated checks support but do not replace observation. |

Small samples and short pilots are starting points, not pass thresholds. Match
the sample to the risk, disclose the gaps, and do not make statistical claims
from a handful of participants. Rare but critical services may legitimately be
infrequently used; do not impose daily-activity targets on them.
